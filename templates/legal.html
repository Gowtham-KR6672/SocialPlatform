Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ updated }}

{{ company }} ("we", "the service") is a social media management tool. Businesses use it to plan and publish content to their own social media accounts and to review comments and performance of that content. This policy explains what data we process and why.

Information we collect

  • Account information you provide: username, display name, email and phone (optional).
  • Connected social accounts: when you click Connect and approve access on Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, X, LinkedIn, Threads, TikTok or Pinterest, we receive an access token and basic profile details (account ID, username or page name) for the account you choose to connect.
  • Content you create: videos, images, captions, hashtags and schedules you upload or write.
  • Performance and engagement data for posts published through the service: views, likes, shares, comment counts, and the text and author name of comments on those posts.

How we use it

  • To publish the content you schedule to the accounts you connected, at the time you choose.
  • To show you comments on your posts in one inbox and let you reply to them.
  • To show analytics and reports about your own posts.
  • To send you notifications and alerts you have enabled (for example, a failed post).

We only access data from accounts you connected yourself, and only for the features above. We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties except the service providers that host the service (application hosting, database and file storage) and, if enabled by your administrator, an AI provider used to draft captions from your own content.

Storage and security

Data is stored on secured cloud infrastructure. Access tokens are used only by the service to call the platforms' official APIs on your behalf and are never shown to other users.

Retention and your choices

  • You can disconnect any social account at any time in Setup; its access token is deleted immediately.
  • You can also remove the app's access from the platform itself (for example Instagram → Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites).
  • To delete your data entirely, follow the steps on the Data Deletion page.
